# Community expectations

Help people build and understand the project. Be respectful, discuss technical
ideas with evidence, and welcome contributors with different experience levels.
Assume good intent while staying open to corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publishing someone
else's private data or credentials are not acceptable. Keep issues and pull
requests relevant to the project and avoid personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, close unproductive threads,
or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should consider
the behavior, its impact, and whether it is repeated.

Report concerns privately through an established maintainer contact channel.
A public project contact will be listed once the repository is published.
Do not include private reports in unrelated public issue threads.
